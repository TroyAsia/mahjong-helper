# Mahjong Helper — Demo Script

**Audience:** people who want to learn American mahjong  
**Length:** about 6–8 minutes  
**Goal:** show that this is a teaching app first (lessons), with safe practice against AI and Charleston help

Open the app to the home screen before you start. Use **Beginner / Easy** everywhere unless noted.

---

## Opening (30 seconds)

**Say:**
> Mahjong Helper teaches American mahjong step by step. You learn with interactive lessons, then practice against AI with hints and undo, and you can train the Charleston separately. Everything runs in the browser. No account.

**Show:** Home — tile mark, **Mahjong Helper**, short tagline, three buttons: **Play vs AI**, **Lessons**, **Charleston**.

---

## 1. Lessons first (about 3 minutes)

This is the main teaching feature. Spend the most time here.

### Steps

1. Click **Lessons**.
2. Click **Beginner**.
3. Point at the lesson list and progress (`N of M complete`).
4. Open **The three suits**.
5. On a teach step: tap a glossary word if one has a tooltip (e.g. suit names).
6. Advance until you hit an **identify** or **quiz** step — answer one, click **Check answer**, then **Continue**.
7. Back out (`← Beginner lessons`), open **How to win**.
8. Skim one teach step about matching a card pattern (not free-form melds).
9. If you reach a **play** discard drill: make one choice, show Correct / Not the best move, then stop — do not finish the whole lesson.
10. Back to the lesson list, optionally open **When someone discards** and click **Call** or **Pass** once to show call drills.
11. Click back to Home.

**Say (while doing this):**
> Lessons are ordered. Beginner starts with tiles: suits, dragons, winds, flowers and jokers. Then how winning works on a card. Later lessons put you in short decision drills, like Chess.com puzzles: discard, call, or pass, with instant feedback. Intermediate and Advanced tracks are here too for later strategy.

**Skip:** finishing every quiz, Intermediate/Advanced deep dive.

---

## 2. Play vs AI (about 2.5 minutes)

Show coaching around a real table, not a full wall.

### Steps

1. From Home, click **Play vs AI**.
2. Click **Easy** (slower bots, more forgiving).
3. On **Play vs AI**, point out:
   - Status line (turn / phase / wall)
   - **Pattern meter** (`N tiles away · best: …`)
   - Notice that wins use a **practice card**, not the official NMJL card
4. Click **Show** on **Winning hands guide**. Scroll 2–3 hands. Point at **Closest now** if it appears. Click **Hide**.
5. If it is your turn to draw: click **Draw**, then pick a discard (or click **Hint** first and read the hint).
6. After a discard, if an explanation appears, read one line aloud.
7. Click **Undo** once — show that you can take back a decision (bots rewind with you).
8. If a **Call or pass** prompt appears: point at **Call …**, **Pass**, and **Win on discard** if shown. Pass or call once; do not chase a full win.
9. Point at **Your hand (East)**, opponent tile counts, **Exposed melds**, discard piles.
10. Mention **New game** and **Declare win** exist; do not play out the wall.
11. Click **← Change difficulty** and say Medium/Hard change bot strength from config only. Return Home.

**Say:**
> Practice is a full four-player feel against AI, but built for learning. The pattern meter tells you how close you are to a practice-card hand. Hints and the discard explainer coach why a move helped or hurt. Undo is unlimited at beginner so mistakes stay cheap.

**Skip:** empty wall, hard difficulty, declaring mahjong unless it is one click away.

---

## 3. Charleston trainer (about 1.5 minutes)

Separate from practice games — practice games skip Charleston.

### Steps

1. From Home, click **Charleston**.
2. Click **Show** on **How the Charleston works**. Skim: first round right / across / left, optional second, courtesy.
3. Click **Hide**.
4. Select 3 tiles on **Your hand (East)** and click **Pass 3 tiles** (or the current pass button).
5. Read any coach feedback that appears.
6. If you reach the vote: point at **Continue second** / **Stop after first** — pick one, do not finish every pass.
7. Click **← Home**.

**Say:**
> Before play starts, American mahjong has the Charleston: passing tiles around the table. A lot of beginners find this confusing, so we gave it its own trainer with an in-app guide and feedback after each pass.

---

## Close (20 seconds)

**Say:**
> So the loop is: learn in lessons, practice decisions against AI with a pattern guide and coaching, and drill the Charleston when you need it. All of that is available from these three buttons on the home screen.

Gesture at **Lessons**, **Play vs AI**, **Charleston**. Stop on Home.

| Call or pass UI | If it appears; otherwise describe |

---

## If something stalls

- **No call window:** Fine — say calls appear when a discard can complete a pung/kong in your pattern; show **When someone discards** in lessons instead.
- **Hint empty / explainer off:** Stay on Easy/Beginner; click Hint after a draw.
- **Nervous about live play:** Spend extra 30 seconds in **How to win** and **When someone discards**, then a shorter AI tour (meter + guide + undo only).

---

## Suggested click path (cheat sheet)

```
Home
 → Lessons → Beginner → The three suits (1–2 steps) → back
 → How to win (skim + optional drill) → back → Home
 → Play vs AI → Easy → Show guide → Hint → Draw/Discard → Undo → Home
 → Charleston → Show guide → pass once → Home
 → Close
```
